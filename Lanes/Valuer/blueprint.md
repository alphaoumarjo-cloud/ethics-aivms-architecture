# Valuer Lane — Operational Blueprint  
Version: 1.0  
Ethics_AIVMS — Multi‑Agent System Architecture

The Valuer Lane is the analytical and evaluative engine of Ethics_AIVMS.  
It transforms theoretical ethics models and computational engines into measurable,
quantifiable ethical valuation outputs for AI systems, agents, and multi‑agent environments.

---

## 1. Purpose

To compute, score, and evaluate ethical load, systemic risk, adversarial pressure,
and governance fragility across AI and multi‑agent systems using structured valuation
frameworks and simulation‑based stress testing.

The Valuer Lane converts abstract ethical theory into **numerical, reportable, and
regulatory‑ready valuation metrics**.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

- Translate academic ethical theory into measurable valuation metrics.
- Run adversarial, stress‑test, and multi‑agent simulations.
- Compute ethical load, systemic risk, and governance fragility scores.
- Generate valuation dashboards, reports, and regulatory briefs.
- Interface with Builder Lane engines for computational scoring.
- Deliver final valuation outputs to the Trainer Lane for public education.

---

## 3. Modules

### **Models/**
Ethical valuation models, scoring frameworks, computational logic, and metric definitions.

### **Scenarios/**
Stress tests, adversarial setups, multi‑agent simulations, and risk environments.

### **Finance/**
Economic analogs, systemic risk models, ethical credit scoring, and governance economics.

### **Valuation/**
Final valuation outputs, dashboards, scoring summaries, and regulatory‑ready reports.

### **Corporate/**
Governance briefs, compliance structures, organizational risk analysis, and audit artifacts.

---

## 4. Data Flows

### **Inbound**
- Academic Lane → theoretical models, ethical frameworks, definitions.
- Builder Lane → computational engines, simulation outputs, agent behaviors.

### **Outbound**
- Trainer Lane → valuation reports, dashboards, public‑education summaries.
- Governance stakeholders → regulatory briefs, compliance artifacts.

---

## 5. Integration Points

- **Academic → Valuer**  
  Converts theory into measurable valuation metrics.

- **Builder → Valuer**  
  Provides simulation data, agent behaviors, and computational scoring.

- **Valuer → Trainer**  
  Supplies valuation outputs for public‑facing education and awareness.

---

## 6. Outputs

- Ethical valuation reports  
- Risk dashboards  
- Scenario analyses  
- Governance briefs  
- Regulatory‑ready scoring summaries
