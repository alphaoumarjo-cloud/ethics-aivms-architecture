# Academic Lane — Operational Blueprint  
Version: 1.0  
Ethics_AIVMS — Multi‑Agent System Architecture

The Academic Lane is the theoretical foundation of Ethics_AIVMS.  
It defines the ethical frameworks, philosophical models, valuation principles,
and governance theories that power the entire multi‑agent system.

---

## 1. Purpose

To establish the ethical, philosophical, and theoretical grounding for all
computational, valuation, and educational operations within Ethics_AIVMS.

The Academic Lane ensures that every model, simulation, and valuation is rooted
in **rigorous ethical theory**.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

- Define ethical frameworks and philosophical foundations.
- Produce theoretical models for valuation and simulation.
- Maintain definitions, axioms, and ethical constructs.
- Provide theoretical updates to Builder and Valuer Lanes.
- Ensure system-wide alignment with ethical principles.

---

## 3. Modules

### **Theory/**
Ethical frameworks, philosophical models, definitions, axioms, and conceptual structures.

### **Models/**
Formal ethical models, valuation theory, governance constructs, and systemic ethics.

### **Definitions/**
Glossaries, terminology, ethical constructs, and conceptual clarity artifacts.

### **Research/**
Academic references, literature reviews, citations, and theoretical expansions.

---

## 4. Data Flows

### **Inbound**
- External academic research  
- Ethical literature  
- Governance theory  
- Philosophical models  

### **Outbound**
- Builder Lane → theoretical models for computational implementation  
- Valuer Lane → ethical frameworks for valuation scoring  
- Trainer Lane → theoretical context for educational modules  

---

## 5. Integration Points

- **Academic → Builder**  
  Supplies theoretical models for computational engines.

- **Academic → Valuer**  
  Provides ethical frameworks for valuation scoring.

- **Academic → Trainer**  
  Provides conceptual grounding for public education.

---

## 6. Outputs

- Ethical frameworks  
- Theoretical models  
- Definitions and terminology  
- Governance theory  
- Research artifacts  

---
