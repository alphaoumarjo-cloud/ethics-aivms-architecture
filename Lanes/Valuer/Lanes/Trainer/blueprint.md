# Trainer Lane — Operational Blueprint  
Version: 1.0  
Ethics_AIVMS — Multi‑Agent System Architecture

The Trainer Lane is the public‑facing education, awareness, and communication engine
of Ethics_AIVMS. It transforms valuation outputs, risk dashboards, and ethical scoring
into accessible, narrative‑driven content for humans, organizations, and regulatory bodies.

---

## 1. Purpose

To convert complex ethical valuation data into clear, digestible, and actionable
educational content for public audiences, stakeholders, and governance entities.

The Trainer Lane ensures that the outputs of the system are **understood**,  
**interpreted correctly**, and **applied responsibly**.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

- Translate valuation outputs into human‑readable educational content.
- Build awareness modules, tutorials, and narrative explanations.
- Create public‑facing dashboards, summaries, and ethical risk stories.
- Interface with Valuer Lane for updated scoring and risk outputs.
- Support governance, compliance, and public education initiatives.

---

## 3. Modules

### **Education/**
Tutorials, explainers, narrative modules, and public‑facing ethical content.

### **Awareness/**
Risk stories, ethical scenarios, societal impact narratives, and outreach materials.

### **Public/**
Dashboards, summaries, visualizations, and communication artifacts.

### **Governance/**
Compliance education, regulatory interpretation guides, and stakeholder briefings.

---

## 4. Data Flows

### **Inbound**
- Valuer Lane → valuation reports, dashboards, scoring summaries.
- Academic Lane → theoretical context for educational framing.

### **Outbound**
- Public audiences → awareness content, dashboards, explainers.
- Governance bodies → compliance guides, regulatory interpretation materials.

---

## 5. Integration Points

- **Valuer → Trainer**  
  Supplies valuation outputs for translation into public‑facing content.

- **Academic → Trainer**  
  Provides theoretical grounding for educational modules.

- **Trainer → Public/Governance**  
  Delivers final awareness and compliance content.

---

## 6. Outputs

- Educational modules  
- Awareness campaigns  
- Public dashboards  
- Ethical risk explainers  
- Governance interpretation guides  

---
