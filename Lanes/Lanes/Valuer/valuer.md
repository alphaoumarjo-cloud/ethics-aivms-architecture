# Valuer Lane Manifest

The Valuer Lane is the analytical and evaluative core of Ethics_AIVMS. It applies
the theoretical models from the Academic Lane and the engines from the Builder Lane
to compute ethical load, systemic risk, adversarial pressure, and governance fragility
for AI systems and multi‑agent environments.

## Purpose
To measure, quantify, and evaluate ethical risk across AI models, agents, and
multi‑agent systems using structured valuation frameworks.

## Contents
- **Models/** — Ethical valuation models, scoring frameworks, and computational logic.
- **Scenarios/** — Stress tests, adversarial setups, and multi‑agent simulations.
- **Finance/** — Economic analogs, systemic risk models, and ethical credit scoring.
- **Valuation/** — Final valuation outputs, reports, and scoring summaries.
- **Corporate/** — Governance briefs, compliance structures, and organizational risk analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Convert academic theory into measurable valuation metrics.
- Run simulations and stress tests to evaluate ethical fragility.
- Produce valuation reports for regulators, industry, and public stakeholders.
- Interface with Builder engines for computational scoring.
- Provide final ethical valuations to the Trainer Lane for public education.

## Outputs
- Ethical valuation reports
- Risk scores and dashboards
- Scenario analyses
- Governance briefs
