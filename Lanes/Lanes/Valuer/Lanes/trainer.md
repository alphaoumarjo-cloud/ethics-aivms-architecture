# Trainer Lane Manifest

The Trainer Lane is the public‑facing educational and communication arm of
Ethics_AIVMS. It transforms valuation outputs, governance briefs, and academic
research into accessible content for the public, regulators, industry, and
multi‑agent system operators.

## Purpose
To educate, inform, and empower the public and stakeholders by translating
complex ethical valuation concepts into clear, digestible, and actionable
learning materials.

## Contents
- **Curriculum/** — Structured lessons, modules, and educational pathways.
- **Mythbusters/** — Public‑facing content debunking misconceptions about AI ethics,
  valuation, systemic risk, and multi‑agent systems.
- **Reels/** — Short‑form educational videos, animations, and social media content
  designed for rapid public awareness.

## Responsibilities
- Convert valuation outputs into public‑friendly explanations.
- Build curriculum for regulators, industry, and general audiences.
- Create myth‑buster content to counter misinformation.
- Produce reels and short‑form content for rapid awareness.
- Serve as the communication bridge between Ethics_AIVMS and the public.

## Outputs
- Educational modules
- Myth‑buster posts
- Reels and short‑form videos
- Public awareness campaigns
