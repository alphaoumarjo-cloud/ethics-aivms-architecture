# Academic Lane Manifest

The Academic Lane is the research backbone of Ethics_AIVMS. It contains all
the theoretical, experimental, and blueprint-level work that defines the
ethical valuation model system.

## Purpose
To develop, test, and refine the scientific foundations of AI ethical valuation.

## Contents
- **Papers/** — Formal research papers, whitepapers, and academic briefs.
- **Experiments/** — Experimental setups, test results, and reproducible studies.
- **Blueprints/** — Architectural diagrams, conceptual models, and MAS design logic.

## Responsibilities
- Establish the theoretical basis for valuation models.
- Document ethical load, systemic risk, and adversarial pressure.
- Provide reproducible research pipelines.
- Supply validated models to the Valuer and Builder lanes.

## Outputs
- Research papers
- Experimental datasets
- Blueprint diagrams
- MAS conceptual frameworks
