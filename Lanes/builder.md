# Builder Lane Manifest

The Builder Lane is the engineering and operational backbone of Ethics_AIVMS.
It transforms academic theory into functional systems, engines, and automation
pipelines that support valuation, governance, and multi‑agent synthesis.

## Purpose
To construct the computational, infrastructural, and automation components that
enable Ethics_AIVMS to operate as a scalable, reproducible, and auditable system.

## Contents
- **Engines/** — Core valuation engines, scoring modules, and computational logic.
- **Infrastructure/** — System scaffolding, environment setup, pipelines, and tooling.
- **Automation/** — Scripts, workflows, CI/CD logic, and operational automations.

## Responsibilities
- Convert academic blueprints into working systems.
- Build valuation engines used by the Valuer Lane.
- Maintain infrastructure for experiments and simulations.
- Automate repetitive or high‑risk operational tasks.
- Ensure reproducibility, stability, and scalability.

## Outputs
- Operational engines
- Infrastructure modules
- Automation workflows
- System scaffolding for MAS lanes
