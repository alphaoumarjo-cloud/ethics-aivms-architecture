# Trainer Lane Manifest

The Trainer Lane governs all learning, refinement, and performance‑shaping
processes inside Ethics_AIVMS. It transforms raw engines, academic logic,
and valuation modules into optimized, adaptive, and reliable multi‑agent
systems.

## Purpose
To train, tune, and refine MAS components so they behave predictably,
ethically, and effectively across diverse environments and valuation tasks.

## Contents
- **Training Pipelines/** — Structured workflows for model and agent training.
- **Datasets/** — Curated academic, valuation, and behavioral datasets.
- **Evaluation Scripts/** — Tools for measuring training progress.
- **Optimization Modules/** — Hyperparameter tuning, reinforcement logic,
  and performance shaping.

## Responsibilities
- Train academic engines derived from the Academic Lane.
- Train valuation engines produced by the Builder Lane.
- Generate behavioral profiles for MAS agents.
- Run reinforcement learning cycles.
- Validate training outcomes against ethical and academic standards.

## Outputs
- Trained models
- Behavioral agent profiles
- Optimization reports
- Training logs
- Performance dashboards

## Integration Points
Trainer Lane integrates with:
- **Academic Lane** → receives academic logic and curriculum structures.
- **Builder Lane** → receives operational engines to train.
- **Valuer Lane** → sends trained models for scoring and evaluation.
