# Valuer Lane Manifest

The Valuer Lane is the analytical and evaluative core of Ethics_AIVMS. It
applies academic theory and builder‑constructed engines to compute ethical
load, systemic risk, adversarial pressure, and governance fragility across
AI systems and multi‑agent environments.

## Purpose
To measure, quantify, and evaluate ethical risk using structured valuation
frameworks, computational scoring models, and multi‑agent simulations.

## Contents
- **Models/** — Ethical valuation models, scoring frameworks, computational logic.
- **Scenarios/** — Stress tests, adversarial setups, multi‑agent simulations.
- **Finance/** — Economic analogs, systemic risk models, ethical credit scoring.
- **Valuation/** — Final valuation outputs, reports, scoring summaries.
- **Corporate/** — Governance briefs, compliance structures, organizational risk analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Convert academic theory into measurable valuation metrics.
- Run simulations and stress tests to evaluate ethical fragility.
- Produce valuation reports for regulators, industry, and public stakeholders.
- Interface with Builder engines for computational scoring.
- Provide final ethical valuations to the Trainer Lane for public education.

## Outputs
- Ethical valuation reports
- Risk scores and dashboards
- Scenario analyses
- Governance briefs
